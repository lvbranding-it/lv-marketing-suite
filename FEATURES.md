# LV Marketing Suite — What's In It

A plain-language index of every app and feature, grouped by what you'd actually use it for.
One line each. For the technical detail — tables, edge functions, design decisions —
see [APP_INVENTORY.md](APP_INVENTORY.md).

*Current as of 25 September 2026. 94 routes · 39 edge functions · 108 database tables.*

---

## Winning the work

| | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **Contacts** `/contacts` | The CRM. Tags, pipeline stages, follow-up dates, deal values, and a research queue. Sorted by most recent activity so new enquiries surface first. |
| **Lead Forms** `/lead-forms` | Shareable public funnels — seven service intake wizards plus the calculator, each in English and Spanish — with views, leads and conversion rate per form. |
| **Website Opportunity Audit** `/tools/website-opportunity-audit` | A public self-serve tool: a prospect enters their site, gets a real audit back. Works as a lead magnet and as the opening of a sales conversation. |
| **Campaign Investment Calculator** `/campaign-investment-calculator` | A public calculator that lets a prospect size their own budget before they ever speak to you. |
| **Service landing pages** | Seven SEO and paid-media landing pages — brand strategy, AV production, web solutions, UX/UI, creative content, photo/video, digital marketing — each in English and Spanish. |
| **Intake** `/intake` | Structured onboarding questionnaires sent to a client by link, so briefing doesn't happen over email threads. |

## Marketing you send out

| | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **Email Campaigns** `/campaigns` | Compose from reusable blocks, send to a segment, and track delivery. Bounces and unsubscribes feed the suppression list automatically, and hard bounces are separated from temporary failures. |
| **Social Publisher** `/social-publisher` | Plan a message once, adapt it per network, publish with control over what goes where. |
| **Contests** `/contests` | Public voting contests for clients, with voter verification and an embeddable widget for their own site. |
| **QR Generator** `/qr-generator` | Branded QR codes with your logo in the middle, for print and signage. |
| **Email Signature Generator** `/email-signature-generator` | Consistent branded signatures for everyone on the team. |

## Making the work

| | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **LV Creative Canvas™** `/dashboard/creative-canvas` | A visual workspace for creative direction: cards for references, prompts and generated images, arrows that carry context or sequence, brand context applied automatically, and PNG/PDF export. |
| **LV Creative Command System™** | 141 slash commands inside the Canvas, 82 live today. Typed, searchable, and configured before anything billable runs — covering people, photography, product, brand, copy and 35 UGC commands. |
| **Skills** `/skills` | 33 single-shot AI marketing specialists — SEO audit, page CRO, copywriting, paid ads, pricing strategy and so on. Each asks a few questions and returns a finished piece of thinking. |
| **Agents** `/agents` | Nine AI agents that hold a brief rather than answer one question: lead intel, strategy, offer building, proposals, content systems, production, website audit, client comms, project management. Results export to PDF or Word. |
| **Image Studio** `/image-studio` | Quick image prep — crop, fill, resize, set a background — without opening a design tool. |
| **Motion Palette** `/motion-palette` | Load a Lottie animation, see every colour it uses, and recolour it to a brand palette. |

## A note on the two AI systems

They get confused, so: **Skills** are specialists you invoke for one job and get one answer.
**Agents** hold a brief across a whole project and produce a structured deliverable, carrying a
brand snapshot between runs. **Commands** are different again — they live inside the Creative
Canvas and act on the cards in front of you.
