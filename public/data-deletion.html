Social Publisher: your Meta data and how to delete it

Social Publisher is part of LV Branding's marketing suite. The LV Branding team uses it to plan and publish posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts it manages. It connects through Meta's official login, as the app "LV Social Publisher".

LV Branding LLC, Houston, Texas. Last updated October 2, 2026.

What Social Publisher receives from Meta

When someone on the team connects Meta, Social Publisher stores:

  • An access token for the connection, the list of permissions granted, and when the token expires.
  • For each Facebook Page the person chose to share: the Page's ID, name and profile picture address, the tasks the person can do on it, and the Page's access token.
  • For each Instagram professional account linked to those Pages: its ID, username, name and profile picture address.

It does not store your Facebook profile, and it does not read messages, comments, followers or insights. It does not sell or share this information.

What Social Publisher creates

  • The posts the team writes in it: captions, internal notes, schedules and the images or videos uploaded for them.
  • Publishing results: the ID and link of each published post, and any error Meta returned.
  • An activity history of who connected, disconnected, scheduled or published, and when.

How it is kept

  • Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored, are used only on LV Branding's servers, and are never sent to anyone's browser.
  • Only members of the LV Branding workspace can see the connected accounts and posts.
  • Uploaded images and videos are kept at hard-to-guess public web addresses, because Meta fetches them from there when it publishes.
  • Everything is kept while the connection is in use, until it is disconnected or a deletion request is made.

How to delete your data

  1. Disconnect in Social Publisher A workspace administrator can open Social Publisher, choose Connections, then Disconnect. The stored access tokens are deleted at once, and Social Publisher can no longer reach your Pages or Instagram accounts.
  2. Remove the app from Facebook On Facebook, open Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations (or Apps and websites). Find "LV Social Publisher" and remove it. This withdraws every permission the app was given.
  3. Ask us to delete the rest Write to admin@lvbranding.com or use the LV Branding contact page, with the subject "Data Deletion Request - Social Publisher". Name the Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts concerned. We will delete their account records and connection history, and the posts and media stored for them, and confirm by email once it is done.
